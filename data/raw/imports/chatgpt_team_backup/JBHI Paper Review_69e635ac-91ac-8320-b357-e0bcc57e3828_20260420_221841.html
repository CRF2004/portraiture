# Research Review Request - JBHI Paper

## Target Venue

JBHI (IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics)

## Title

Temperature-Controlled Knowledge Distillation for Interpretable Multi-Label ECG Diagnosis

---

## Core Problem

Deep learning models achieve high accuracy in ECG diagnosis but lack interpretability for clinical deployment. Knowledge distillation can transfer knowledge from complex teachers to interpretable students, but the optimal soft-label setting (temperature) for multi-label medical diagnosis remains unclear.

---

## Methodology

- Teacher: XGBoost ensembles (58 models for 56+ diseases)
- Student: Decision trees (one per disease, shallow depth ~9.4)
- Temperature sweep: T ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8} with leakage-free validation-based selection
- Dataset: PTB-XL (21,799 samples, 56 diseases)
- External validation: CODE-15 (8,000 samples, 5 mapped diagnoses - 8.9% coverage)
