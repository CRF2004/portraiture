## 🤖 Assistant (2026/6/6 13:42:09)

JBHI 方法学论文的 **Introduction 末尾 contribution** 一般不是单纯写“我们做了实验、效果很好”，而是要回答：

> 现有方法缺什么？  
> 我们提出了什么可复用的方法？  
> 这个方法如何被严格验证？  
> 它为什么对 biomedical / health informatics 有意义？

比较推荐写成 **一小段承接 + 3–4 条贡献**。

---

## 1. Contribution 段落的常见结构

可以这样组织：

**承接句：**

> To address these limitations, we propose XXX, a methodological framework for XXX.

然后列贡献：

1. **方法框架贡献**  
   提出一个新的 framework / protocol / audit design，而不是只说“我们应用了某个方法”。

2. **操作化贡献**  
   把抽象问题变成可执行、可检验的步骤，比如 evidence units、matched controls、specificity score、tier rule。

3. **验证设计贡献**  
   说明你不是只在一个模型或一个数据集上跑了一下，而是有 operator battery、cross-dataset transfer、cross-architecture consistency 等验证机制。

4. **医学场景实例化贡献**  
   用 ECG 作为典型场景展示方法如何落地，但不要让读者觉得这只是 ECG trick。

---

## 2. 对你这篇论文，推荐的 contribution 逻辑

你的论文最好不要写成：

> Our contributions are:  
> 1) We audit ECG classifiers.  
> 2) We test three architectures.  
> 3) We use CODE and PTB-XL.  
> 4) We obtain three evidence tiers.

这样会显得像实验罗列。

更适合写成：

> Our contributions are:  
> 1) We introduce a matched-control perturbation audit framework...  
> 2) We formalize pathway-specific evidence use as a falsifiable comparison between target evidence units and matched controls...  
> 3) We develop an operator validity battery to prevent perturbation artifacts from driving conclusions...  
> 4) We instantiate and stress-test the framework in 12-lead ECG classification under cross-dataset and cross-architecture settings...

这个更像 **methodology paper**。

---

## 3. 可直接用于你论文的英文版本

你可以考虑这个版本：

> **The main contributions of this study are fourfold.**  
> First, we propose a matched-control pathway audit framework for evaluating whether deep learning models rely on domain-relevant evidence rather than merely achieving high discrimination. The framework converts clinical knowledge into pre-specified target evidence units and anatomically or temporally matched controls, enabling evidence-use claims to be tested as falsifiable perturbation comparisons.  
> Second, we introduce an anatomical specificity criterion, quantified by the Anatomical Specificity Score, in which a pathway claim is supported only when perturbing the target evidence unit is more disruptive than perturbing the strongest matched control. This design separates target sensitivity from evidence-use specificity.  
> Third, we incorporate a perturbation-operator validity battery to audit the perturbation mechanism itself and to reduce the risk that explanation conclusions are driven by operator-induced artifacts rather than model evidence use.  
> Fourth, we instantiate the framework in 12-lead ECG classification and evaluate its stability across datasets, diagnostic pathways, and model architectures, yielding pathway-level evidence tiers that distinguish pathway-supported, evidence-limited, and unsupported model behavior.

这个版本的优点是：

- 第一条讲 **framework**；
- 第二条讲 **metric / decision rule**；
- 第三条讲 **operator audit**；
- 第四条讲 **ECG instantiation + validation**；
- 没有过度声称“first”；
- 也没有把 contribution 写成结果复述。

---

## 4. 你这篇最应该避免的写法

避免这种：

> We make the following contributions:  
> 1) We propose a new ECG explainability method.  
> 2) We compare zero-masking, interpolation, and inpainting.  
> 3) We evaluate ResNet, SE-ResNet, and Transformer.  
> 4) We show that some models are pathway-supported.

问题是：  
这会让审稿人觉得你的贡献是 **ECG XAI 实验组合**，而不是 **医学深度学习证据使用审计方法论**。

你真正要突出的是：

> 我们不是提出一个更好看的 explanation，而是提出一个可以审计 explanation 是否支持医学证据通路声明的 matched-control methodology。

---

## 5. 推荐的 Introduction 末尾整体写法

可以按这个顺序：

**Paragraph 1：gap summary**

> Existing perturbation-based explanations often quantify whether predictions are sensitive to clinically relevant regions, but they rarely test whether such sensitivity is specific relative to matched controls or stable under dataset shift.

**Paragraph 2：your solution**

> To address this gap, we propose a matched-control pathway audit framework...

**Paragraph 3：contributions**

> The main contributions are fourfold...

这样 Introduction 结尾会非常清楚：  
**问题 → 方法 → 贡献**，而不是突然列实验。

## 🤖 Assistant (2026/6/6 13:45:00)

JBHI（IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics）的方法学论文里，**Introduction 最后的 contribution 部分**一般写得比较直接，通常放在“研究缺口/现有方法不足”之后，用一段话或 3–4 条 bullet 总结本文的核心贡献。

常见逻辑是：

> **问题背景 → 现有方法不足 → 本文提出的方法 → 主要贡献**

Contribution 不只是说“我们做了什么”，而是要强调：

1. **方法学新颖性**：提出了什么新的模型、框架、算法、机制或学习策略。  
2. **技术上解决了什么关键问题**：比如数据异质性、小样本、标签噪声、跨中心泛化、可解释性、多模态融合、隐私保护等。  
3. **验证是否充分**：在哪些公开/真实临床数据集、多中心数据、外部验证集上验证。  
4. **临床或生物医学意义**：该方法为什么对 biomedical / health informatics 场景有价值。

---

### 常用写法

可以这样开头：

> The main contributions of this work are summarized as follows:

然后列 3–4 点：

```text
1) We propose a novel [method/framework/model] for [task], which addresses [specific limitation] by [key technical idea].

2) We introduce [module/strategy/mechanism], enabling [benefit], such as improved robustness, interpretability, generalization, or efficiency.

3) We conduct comprehensive experiments on [datasets/cohorts], including [external validation/cross-center validation/ablation studies], demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed method.

4) We provide clinical/biomedical insights by [interpretability analysis/feature attribution/risk stratification/case study], supporting the potential utility of the method in [application scenario].
```

---

### 更像 JBHI 风格的英文模板

```text
The main contributions of this study are threefold.

First, we propose a novel [framework name] for [biomedical task], which explicitly models [key challenge] and overcomes the limitations of existing methods in [specific aspect].

Second, we design a [module/mechanism/learning strategy] to enhance [robustness/generalization/interpretability/multimodal representation], making the model more suitable for real-world biomedical data characterized by [noise/heterogeneity/missingness/domain shift].

Third, extensive experiments on [number] datasets/cohorts demonstrate that the proposed method consistently outperforms representative baseline methods. Additional ablation studies and visualization analyses further validate the effectiveness and interpretability of the proposed components.
```

---

### 中文理解版

贡献部分最好不要写成：

> We used deep learning to predict disease risk.

这太泛。

应该写成：

> We propose a domain-adaptive multimodal learning framework for disease risk prediction, which mitigates cross-center distribution shifts by aligning latent representations across heterogeneous clinical cohorts.

也就是说，要从“用了什么方法”提升到“**提出了什么机制来解决什么问题**”。

---

### 一个完整示例

假设你的论文是关于医学时间序列预测的：

```text
The main contributions of this work are summarized as follows.

1) We propose a novel temporal-aware representation learning framework for clinical time-series prediction, which explicitly captures irregular sampling patterns and long-range dependencies in electronic health records.

2) We introduce a missingness-guided attention mechanism to jointly model observed values and missing patterns, thereby improving the robustness of prediction under incomplete clinical data.

3) We evaluate the proposed method on two public ICU datasets and one real-world hospital cohort. The experimental results demonstrate consistent improvements over state-of-the-art baselines, and ablation studies confirm the contribution of each proposed component.

4) We provide interpretability analyses to identify clinically relevant temporal patterns, supporting the potential use of the proposed framework in decision-support scenarios.
```

### 写 JBHI contribution 时的几个注意点

Contribution 通常控制在 **3 条左右**，最多 4 条。每条不要太长，但要具体。

尽量避免这些表达：

```text
We are the first to...
To the best of our knowledge...
Our method achieves the best performance...
```

除非你非常确定。更稳妥的写法是：

```text
This study makes the following contributions...
We propose...
We design...
We demonstrate...
We validate...
```

方法学论文里，contribution 的核心不是“性能提升了多少”，而是：

> **提出了什么新方法，解决了什么 biomedical/clinical data challenge，并且通过充分实验验证其有效性。**
