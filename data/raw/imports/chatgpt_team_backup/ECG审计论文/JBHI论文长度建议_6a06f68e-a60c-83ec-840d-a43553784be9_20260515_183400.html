# 建议的新大纲：压缩版 JBHI 主文结构

## Title

Beyond Discrimination: Matched-Control Pathway Auditing for ECG Classifiers

标题可以更短一点。现在标题没问题，但略长。

---

## Abstract

结构建议：

1. Problem：ECG classifiers can be accurate but may rely on non-specific signal regions.
2. Method：We propose a matched-control perturbation audit with predefined disease pathways, anatomical controls, operator documentation, and external transfer.
3. Key results：
- 1AVB: target PR perturbation large, but QRS control stronger → false-positive pathway claim avoided.
- BBB: QRS pathway supported across architectures and external dataset.
- AFIB: zero-masking positive disappears under linear interpolation → operator artifact.
- SBRAD: architecture-dependent.
4. Conclusion：Discrimination metrics are insufficient; pathway-specific evidence requires matched controls and operator-aware validation.

你现在 Abstract 信息量太大，尤其结果部分塞了太多数字。保留 4 个结论就够了。当前摘要已经把 1AVB、BBB、SBRAD、AFIB 全塞进去了，读起来像 Results 压缩包。fileciteturn0file0

# I. Introduction

目标：2 页以内。

## A. Motivation: High discrimination is not pathway evidence

写清楚：

- ECG deep models AUROC 很高。
- 但高 AUROC 不说明模型用的是临床一致区域。
- 例如 1AVB 可能不是靠 PR，而是靠 QRS 或其他 shortcut。

## B. Gap: Existing XAI lacks specificity tests

不要展开成大综述，只讲三个 gap：

1. target-only perturbation 只能证明 sensitivity，不能证明 specificity；
2. 很少使用 anatomically matched controls；
3. 很少验证解释结论是否 survives dataset shift 和 perturbation operator change。

## C. Contribution

建议列 4 条：

1. 提出 matched-control pathway audit；
2. 定义 ASS / CDDC / evidence tiers；
3. 引入 operator-aware protocol，避免 zero-masking artifact；
4. 在五个 ECG diagnosis、三种 raw-signal architectures、两个 datasets 上验证。

这里不要再提前讲太多具体结果。

## V. Discussion

### Opening paragraph：总起，不单独成节

## A. Matched Controls Convert Sensitivity Into Specificity

这一节讲 1AVB。

核心观点：

- target perturbation 只能证明“这个区域有用”；
- matched-control 才能证明“这个区域比合理对照更特异”；
- 1AVB 是关键例子：PR perturbation 很大，但 QRS control 更大；
- 所以 target-only perturbation 会产生 false-positive pathway claim。

这一节可以写成 1–2 段，不要再复述所有数字。

建议表达：

> The 1AVB case illustrates why matched controls are not a technical refinement but a requirement for pathway claims. PR-window perturbation produced a large and clinically plausible degradation, which would usually be interpreted as evidence that the model uses AV-conduction timing. However, the matched QRS control was more disruptive under the primary operator and on the external dataset. Thus, the model was PR-sensitive but not PR-specific. This distinction is exactly what standard perturbation explanations miss.

---

## B. Operator Choice Is Part of the Claim

这一节讲 AFIB，也可以顺带提 CRBBB。

核心观点：

- perturbation operator 不是中性工具；
- zero-masking 会引入 hard-boundary artifact；
- AFIB under zero-masking 看起来 positive，但 linear interpolation 下消失；
- CRBBB under zero-masking 甚至会造成 external failure 假象；
- 所以 pathway claim 必须说明 operator，并最好做 operator sensitivity。

建议表达：

> The audit also shows that perturbation operators can determine the direction of a pathway conclusion. AFIB appeared to have cross-dataset atrial-window support under zero-masking, but this signal disappeared under the continuity-preserving primary operator. Conversely, CRBBB looked transfer-stressed under zero-masking but became robustly pathway-supported under linear interpolation. These reversals show that the perturbation operator is part of the explanation claim, not a secondary implementation detail.

这一节不要展开“linear interpolation 为什么最好”，因为 Methods 已经说过。Discussion 只讲意义。

---

## C. Pathway Evidence Should Be Assigned Per Model

这一节讲 BBB 和 SBRAD。

核心观点：

- BBB 是强阳性：三种 architecture 都 supported；
- SBRAD 是模型依赖：ResNet EL，SE-ResNet / Transformer NS；
- 所以不要把一个 architecture 的解释结论泛化为 disease-level claim；
- evidence tier 应该 per architecture-disease pair。

建议表达：

> The cross-architecture audit suggests that pathway evidence should be assigned to models, not diseases in the abstract. QRS-morphology evidence for CRBBB and CLBBB was stable across ResNet, SE-ResNet, and Transformer, making it the strongest positive finding in the study. In contrast, SBRAD rate-pathway evidence was architecture-dependent: ResNet showed directionally replicated but statistically limited support, whereas SE-ResNet and Transformer were not supported. This heterogeneity argues against reporting a single disease-level explanation without specifying the audited model.

## D. Scope and Limitations

把原来的 limitations 合并进 Discussion 最后一个小节，别再单独写很长。

保留 4–5 个限制：

- 只有两个数据集，一个外部验证集；
- CODE labels 是 automated labels；
- perturbation 不是完美 physiological counterfactual；
- 只覆盖五个 ECG diagnoses；
- 没有 clinician review / prospective validation。

建议表达：

> Several boundaries are important. First, the audit evaluates pathway specificity under predefined perturbation operators; it does not prove mechanistic equivalence to clinician reasoning. Second, CODE-15% labels are automated, and PTB-XL fold 10 only partly buffers label-circularity concerns. Third, linear interpolation is more conservative than zero-masking but remains an imperfect physiological counterfactual. Finally, the audit covers five diagnoses and three raw-signal architectures; broader disease panels, additional external datasets, clinician review, and subgroup analyses are needed before drawing clinical deployment conclusions.
