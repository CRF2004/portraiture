可以说基本正确，但建议把 “the most common choice” 稍微降调。我能找到强支撑说明 zero-masking/setting-to-zero 是 ECG 扰动解释中常用、甚至被作者称为 frequently used 的方案；但如果写成 “the most common choice”，最好有系统综述逐篇统计，否则审稿人可能会追问“most common”的定量依据。

更稳妥的改写：

> Second, the perturbation operator itself is rarely treated as an auditable methodological variable. In ECG XAI, zero-masking, or setting signal samples/segments to zero, is a frequently used occlusion baseline; however, it can introduce abrupt jumps to zero and out-of-distribution noise, whose influence on explanation conclusions is often not explicitly quantified.
